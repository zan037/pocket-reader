Storage and troubleshooting

Imported PDFs and progress stay in this browser's local storage. Avoid clearing the browser's site data if you want to keep your library.

Pocket Reader is a personal web app. The app shell is cached for offline use. The PDF rendering engine is downloaded on first use and cached in this browser where supported; open a book online once before relying on offline reading.
